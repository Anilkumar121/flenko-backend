Common Questions from B2B Buyers & Suppliers

What is Flenko.com and how does it connect wholesale buyers with manufacturers?

Flenko.com is a next-generation global B2B wholesale marketplace that allows businesses, wholesalers, retailers, and institutional buyers to discover and connect directly with certified manufacturers and verified suppliers across India and 190+ countries. Buyers can request quotes (RFQs), negotiate wholesale pricing directly on WhatsApp, and execute protected purchases backed by Flenko Trade Assurance.

How can I request bulk quotes or submit a buying requirement (RFQ)?

Submitting an RFQ is 100% free for buyers on Flenko. Click on the "Post RFQ" button anywhere on the platform, enter your product name, required quantity, target price, and delivery location. Our matching engine distributes your requirement exclusively to verified suppliers in that industry, and you will receive competitive quotes within 24 hours.

How is Flenko different from traditional directories?

While legacy directories share one buyer lead across multiple competing vendors causing spam, Flenko provides direct supplier matching, transparent verified profiles, dedicated storefront website links, multi-country international sourcing pavilions, built-in escrow Trade Assurance, and clean digital CRM tools designed for modern industrial commerce.

How can manufacturers and exporters start selling on Flenko?

Manufacturers, authorized distributors, and OEM/ODM exporters can click "Sell on Flenko", complete their business registration, upload their factory certifications and GST details, and publish their product catalog. Verified Pro suppliers get high-priority Google search indexing and access to high-intent buyer leads.
